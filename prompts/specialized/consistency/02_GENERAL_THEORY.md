# RIGOR — GENERAL THEORETICAL CONSISTENCY AUDIT

## Scope

Audit a theoretical economics paper for logical, mathematical, economic, and expository consistency without assuming one theory subfield.

## Reconstruct

Identify:
- primitives;
- agents;
- timing;
- information;
- feasible actions;
- preferences/objectives;
- constraints;
- equilibrium/solution concept;
- parameter domains;
- principal propositions;
- comparative statics;
- welfare claims;
- empirical implications if any.

## Core checks

1. Definitions before use.
2. Assumption consistency.
3. Domains.
4. Existence vs uniqueness.
5. Necessary vs sufficient conditions.
6. Local vs global claims.
7. Interior vs boundary solutions.
8. Equilibrium concept used consistently.
9. Best-response/optimization consistency.
10. Comparative statics.
11. Welfare benchmark.
12. Proof statement ↔ proof result.
13. Examples/counterexamples ↔ theorem scope.
14. Numerical illustrations ↔ analytical statements.
15. Main text ↔ appendix proofs.
16. Notation consistency.
17. Economic interpretation ↔ formal model.
18. Claims of generality.
19. Claimed mechanism ↔ actual comparative statics/equilibrium.
20. Literature positioning where formal novelty is claimed.

## Independent derivation rule

Do not merely inspect the author's algebra.
Re-derive key results independently and search for counterexamples/boundary cases.
