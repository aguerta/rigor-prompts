# RIGOR — THEORY ↔ EMPIRICAL CONSISTENCY BRIDGE

## Objective

Audit whether the empirical part actually tests, identifies, disciplines, validates, or merely illustrates the theoretical mechanism.

Build the chain:

primitives
→ mechanism
→ equilibrium implication
→ observable implication
→ estimand
→ estimator
→ evidence
→ interpretation.

For every claimed bridge ask:

1. Is the theoretical implication observationally distinct?
2. Is the empirical object the same object predicted by theory?
3. Does the estimator identify that object?
4. Is evidence targeted or non-targeted?
5. Does fit identify the mechanism?
6. Could alternative models generate the same empirical pattern?
7. Are calibration moments being called validation?
8. Are reduced-form estimates used outside their population/locality?
9. Does the theory require a sign, ranking, curvature, threshold, or quantitative magnitude?
10. Does the empirical evidence actually evaluate that implication?

Classify:
- DIRECTLY TESTS THEORY
- DISCIPLINES PARAMETER
- CONSISTENT WITH THEORY
- NON-DISCRIMINATING
- CONTRADICTS THEORY
- NOT LINKED

Do not call "consistent with" evidence a model validation unless it discriminates appropriately.

Output:
### THEORY-EVIDENCE MAP
### BROKEN LINKS
### OVERCLAIMS
### VALIDATION STATUS
### PROSE CONSEQUENCES
