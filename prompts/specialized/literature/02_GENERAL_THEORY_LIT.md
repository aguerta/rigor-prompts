# RIGOR — GENERAL THEORY LITERATURE & CONTRIBUTION AUDIT

Map:
1. closest model class;
2. same primitives;
3. same equilibrium mechanism;
4. same theorem/result;
5. stronger/weaker known results;
6. known counterexamples/limitations;
7. empirical applications;
8. recent theoretical frontier.

Do not treat different notation as novelty.

For each comparator record:
primitives | equilibrium concept | assumptions | main result | proof scope | what current paper adds.

Verify every citation externally.
Never invent DOI, title, author list, journal, or bibliographic fields.

Actively search for papers that already prove the claimed result under equal or weaker assumptions.
